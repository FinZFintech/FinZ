Employee Loan

Liquidity for your salary cycle, without the wait.

A short-tenure salary-linked loan for verified employees. Disbursed to your bank account, repaid automatically from your salary account via eNACH. Useful for emergencies, short-term goals, or bridging between paydays.

Eligibility

Who can apply

Applicant

  • Indian resident employee aged 21–58
  • Minimum 6 months at current employer
  • In-hand monthly salary ≥ {{EMP_MIN_SALARY}}
  • Salary credited to bank account (for eNACH setup)
  • Valid PAN and Aadhaar

Employer

  • Registered Indian company, PSU, government department or approved institution
  • On FinZ Finance's approved employer list, or subject to employer-level verification
  • Company must be current on EPFO / TDS filings for automated income verification
Please note: Not all employers are pre-approved. We verify employment via EPFO / Payroll APIs in accordance with RBI Digital Lending Guidelines.
How repayment works

Auto-debit via eNACH

1. Mandate setup

You authorise eNACH once during onboarding. Your bank approves via OTP/net-banking.

2. EMI deduction

Each month, the EMI is debited from your salary account on the scheduled date.

3. Closure & NOC

On final EMI, your loan is closed and an e-NOC is emailed to you at no cost.
