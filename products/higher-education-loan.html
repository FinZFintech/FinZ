Eligibility

Who can apply

Student

  • Indian citizen aged 18–35
  • Admission confirmed to an approved institution (India / abroad)
  • PAN + Aadhaar for digital KYC
  • Academic record meeting minimum criteria

Co-applicant

  • Parent, guardian or spouse with verifiable income
  • Bureau score and FOIR per internal credit policy
  • Household indebtedness within RBI NBFC-MFI limits
  • Bank account for eNACH mandate
Please note: Final sanction is subject to credit assessment, verification and availability of product as per our Board-approved Credit Policy.
