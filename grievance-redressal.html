Grievance Redressal Mechanism

FinZ Finance Private Limited is committed to providing its customers with the highest standard of service. If you are unhappy with any aspect of our service or product, we want to hear from you. Our three-tier escalation mechanism is designed to ensure that your grievance is addressed fairly and within the timeframes stipulated by the Reserve Bank of India.

Turnaround commitment: We acknowledge grievances within 3 working days and endeavour to resolve them within 15 business days. If we are unable to resolve within 30 days, you may approach the RBI Ombudsman for NBFCs.

Level 1 — Customer Care

Please reach out to our customer care team as a first step. Most queries and complaints are resolved at this stage.

Response timeline: within 7 business days.

Level 2 — Grievance Redressal Officer

If you are not satisfied with the response received from customer care, or have not received a response within 7 business days, you may escalate the matter to our Grievance Redressal Officer.

Level 3 — Principal Nodal Officer

If your complaint remains unresolved after Level 2, you may further escalate to our Principal Nodal Officer. The Principal Nodal Officer is responsible for liaising with the Reserve Bank of India and the Office of the Ombudsman.

Level 4 — Reserve Bank of India Ombudsman

If you are not satisfied with the resolution provided, or have not received a reply from the Company within a period of 30 days of lodging the complaint, you may approach the RBI's Office of the Ombudsman under the Reserve Bank — Integrated Ombudsman Scheme, 2021.

Information required for a complaint

To help us resolve your complaint efficiently, please provide:

  • Full name, registered mobile number and email address
  • Loan application number or loan account number (if already sanctioned)
  • Detailed nature of the grievance and dates of prior communication
  • Copies of relevant documents (loan agreement, SMS / email received, screenshots)
  • The resolution you are seeking

Complaint register

All complaints are logged in a central complaint register. A unique ticket number is provided to the customer for tracking. The Board reviews the functioning of the grievance redressal mechanism at least annually, and a summary is disclosed in the Annual Report.

Your right: At any point, you may refer directly to the RBI Ombudsman without first exhausting internal escalation, although we encourage customers to give us a first opportunity to resolve.
