Code of conduct — what you can expect

  • Recovery agents contact you only between 8:00 AM and 7:00 PM, at a location convenient to you.
  • Agents identify themselves by name, agency and authorisation letter.
  • Agents do not use threats, abusive language or coercive behaviour.
  • Agents do not visit your workplace or contact relatives/friends without your consent, except as legally permitted.
  • Agents do not photograph you or your premises without consent.

Report misconduct

If you believe an agency has violated the above code of conduct, please report it immediately. Misconduct is taken very seriously and results in de-empanelment.
