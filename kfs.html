Key Fact Statement (KFS)

In accordance with the Reserve Bank of India's Key Fact Statement (KFS) for Loans and Advances circular (RBI/2023-24/117 DOR.STR.REC.75/21.02.001/2023-24 dated 15 April 2024, as applicable from 1 October 2024), FinZ Finance Private Limited issues a standardised Key Fact Statement to every prospective borrower before the loan agreement is executed. The KFS lists all key facts in a single, clear format so you can compare and decide with full information.

Your right: A loan must not be disbursed unless you have received, acknowledged and agreed to the KFS. Any charge not listed in the KFS is not payable by you. A three-working-day cooling-off period is available to exit the loan without prepayment penalty.
