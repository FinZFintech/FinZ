Fair Practices Code

Last reviewed by the Board on {{FPC_LAST_REVIEWED}}. This Fair Practices Code (FPC) is adopted in line with the guidelines issued by the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) in the Master Direction – Reserve Bank of India (Non-Banking Financial Company – Scale Based Regulation) Directions, 2023 and the Master Direction – Reserve Bank of India (Regulatory Framework for Microfinance Loans) Directions, 2022.

1. Applications for loans and their processing

  • All communications with borrowers are in a language understood by them.
  • The loan application form contains necessary information that affects the interest of the borrower so that a meaningful comparison with terms and conditions offered by other NBFCs can be made.
  • The application form indicates the documents required to be submitted.
  • An acknowledgement of the receipt of the completed application and indicative timeline for disposal is given.
  • A Key Fact Statement (KFS) is issued to the prospective borrower in a standardised format.

2. Loan appraisal and terms and conditions

  • The Company conveys in writing to the borrower, by means of a sanction letter or otherwise, the amount of loan sanctioned along with the terms and conditions including the annualised rate of interest, method of application, penal charges and the KFS.
  • The Company keeps the acceptance of these terms and conditions by the borrower on its record.
  • Any charges not mentioned in the KFS shall not be charged to the borrower at any stage during the term of the loan.
  • Penal charges levied for non-compliance of material terms and conditions of the loan contract shall be treated as "penal charges" and shall not be levied in the form of "penal interest" that is added to the rate of interest charged on the advances.

3. Disbursement of loans including changes in terms and conditions

  • Any changes in terms and conditions, including disbursement schedule, interest rates, service charges and prepayment charges, are notified to the borrower.
  • Changes in interest rate and charges are effected prospectively and a suitable condition is incorporated in the loan agreement.
  • Decision to recall, accelerate payment, or seek additional securities is in consonance with the loan agreement.
  • All securities are released on repayment of all dues, or on realisation of the outstanding amount of the loan subject to any legitimate right or lien for any other claim the Company may have.

4. General

  • The Company refrains from interference in the affairs of the borrower except for the purposes provided in the terms and conditions of the loan agreement (unless information not earlier disclosed by the borrower has come to its notice).
  • In case of receipt of request from the borrower for transfer of borrower's account, the consent or otherwise — objection, if any — is conveyed within 21 days from the date of receipt of request.
  • In the matter of recovery of loans, the Company does not resort to undue harassment viz. persistently bothering the borrower at odd hours, use of muscle power for recovery of loans, etc. Recovery is conducted during reasonable hours (8:00 AM – 7:00 PM) at locations mutually convenient to the borrower.
  • Staff members are adequately trained to deal with customers in an appropriate manner.

5. Confidentiality

The Company maintains customer confidentiality and does not pass on customer information to outside agencies or individuals without the consent of the borrower, except where such disclosure is required by law or by the courts.

6. Responsibility of the Board of Directors

The Board has laid down the appropriate grievance redressal mechanism within the organisation to resolve disputes arising in this regard. Such a mechanism ensures that all disputes arising out of the decisions of lending institutions' functionaries are heard and disposed of at least at the next higher level. The Board also undertakes a periodic review of the compliance of the Fair Practices Code and the functioning of the grievance redressal mechanism at various levels of management. A consolidated report of such reviews is submitted to the Board annually.

7. Regulation of excessive interest charged

The Board has laid down appropriate internal principles and procedures in determining interest rates and processing and other charges, and ensures that they are not excessive. The Company has adopted an Interest Rate Policy, which is approved by the Board and reviewed annually. The rate of interest to be charged depends on the gradation of risk, cost of funds, operating expenses, and an appropriate margin.

8. Recovery of loans

  • Recovery is based on a co-operative approach with an emphasis on the customer's ability to pay and flexibility in the collection mechanism.
  • Recovery agents are approached only after the borrower is contacted through the Company's own staff, and only when the account is classified as overdue per our Board-approved Recovery Policy.
  • All empanelled agencies are listed on this page. Identity of the agency representative is verifiable by calling our customer care.
  • No agency uses coercion, threats, abusive language, or engages with the borrower at odd hours.

9. Lending in digital mode

Lending through the Company's website, mobile application and authorised Digital Lending Apps (DLAs) follows the RBI Guidelines on Digital Lending dated 2 September 2022:

  • All fees, charges and APR are disclosed in the KFS upfront.
  • A cooling-off period of three working days is provided, during which a borrower may exit the loan without penal charges.
  • Data collection is limited to the information required for the loan and is stored within India.
  • Borrowers have access to all grievance redressal channels listed in the Grievance Redressal Policy.
  • The name of the Lending Service Provider (LSP), if any, is disclosed to the borrower.

10. Language and mode of communication

The FPC is published on the website in English. The Company makes the FPC available in Hindi and at least one regional language commonly used in its area of operation on request. Communications to borrowers — sanction letter, KFS, repayment schedule, notices — are provided in a language understood by the borrower.

11. Grievance redressal

In case of any grievance, please refer to our Grievance Redressal Mechanism. The escalation matrix up to the RBI Ombudsman is clearly laid out.

Related policies: This Fair Practices Code is supplemented by the Interest Rate Policy, KYC & AML Policy, Grievance Redressal Policy and Outsourcing Policy — all available on the Policies & Disclosures page.
