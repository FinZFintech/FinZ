Lending Partners

In line with the Reserve Bank of India's Guidelines on Digital Lending (dated 2 September 2022) and the Co-Lending Model circular, FinZ Finance Private Limited discloses below the regulated entities with whom we have sourcing, co-lending or balance-sheet partnerships. Each partner is itself a Reserve Bank of India regulated entity.

Why this matters: As a borrower, you have the right to know who ultimately lends to you, who collects repayment, and where your data is shared. This page and your Key Fact Statement (KFS) name the lending partner for your specific loan.

Your lending relationship

  • For every loan sanctioned, the Key Fact Statement (KFS) issued to you specifies the name of the lender (which may be FinZ Finance Private Limited or a co-lending partner) and the share of each party's exposure, if applicable.
  • Under co-lending arrangements, both entities share customer-level credit risk in a pre-agreed ratio as per a formal co-lending agreement.
  • All servicing (EMI collection, grievances, NOC issuance) is routed through a single point of contact — FinZ Finance Private Limited — unless otherwise agreed with you in writing.

Grievance responsibility

Regardless of which partner funds your loan, FinZ Finance Private Limited is your first point of grievance contact. Your complaints will be acknowledged and resolved through our Grievance Redressal Mechanism. Where applicable, the complaint is simultaneously reported to the co-lending partner.

Data sharing

Personal and loan data is shared with lending partners strictly on a need-to-know basis for credit decisioning, KYC compliance, credit bureau reporting and servicing. Our Privacy Policy lists all categories of data shared and with whom.

Note: The list of lending partners is updated on this page within 7 days of a new partnership going live or an existing arrangement being terminated.
