How it works

Five steps from application to disbursal.

1. Apply online

Share basic details and consent to Aadhaar-based eKYC.

2. Verification

PAN, bank, income and identity verified digitally.

3. Sanction & KFS

Receive your Key Fact Statement with all fees & APR disclosed.

4. eSign & eNACH

Sign agreement digitally; set up auto-debit for EMIs.

5. Disbursal

Funds credited to your bank account or institution.
