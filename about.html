About Us

Responsible lending, built for a digital India.

FinZ Finance Private Limited is an RBI-registered Non-Banking Financial Company – Micro Finance Institution (NBFC-MFI). We combine modern technology with the discipline of a regulated lender to offer transparent, compliant and customer-friendly credit products.

Our mission

To make formal credit accessible, transparent and respectful for every eligible Indian — starting with education financing and employee liquidity solutions, and scaling to broader microfinance segments over time.

What we stand for

  • Transparent pricing. Every borrower receives a Key Fact Statement (KFS) disclosing the Annualised Percentage Rate (APR), processing fees, insurance charges and recovery charges before signing the loan agreement.
  • Responsible recovery. Collections are governed by our Fair Practices Code and the RBI's Code of Conduct. We do not tolerate coercive or harassing behaviour from any staff member or empanelled agency.
  • Data privacy by design. Customer data is stored and processed only within India in line with the RBI's Digital Lending Guidelines and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.
  • Financial inclusion. As an NBFC-MFI, we focus on low-income households and underserved borrowers, with household-income eligibility and indebtedness caps as mandated by RBI Master Direction on MFIs.
