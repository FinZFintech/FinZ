Policies & Disclosures

All policies of FinZ Finance Private Limited are approved by the Board of Directors and reviewed at least annually. Policies marked in regional languages are provided to enable borrowers in our service areas to read the terms in a language they understand, in line with the RBI's Master Direction on Microfinance Loans.
